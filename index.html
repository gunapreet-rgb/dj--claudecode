Story

Named after the one that doesn't forgive.

K2 is the second-highest mountain on earth and by a wide margin the harder one. No easy route, no good season, no room for gear that almost works.

We started in 2019 after a bottle split on a cold night at 5,300 m and soaked a sleeping bag. The fix took three years and eleven prototypes. Every bottle we ship still gets frozen, dropped, and pressure-cycled before it leaves the building.

"Test it where it fails. Then build the one that doesn't." — the only thing written on our workshop wall
